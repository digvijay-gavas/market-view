WHAT THESE FIGURES MEAN

Saved captures. No live quotes.

Value uses the price saved with each holdings capture. Refresh reads Drive again; it does not capture holdings or fetch market prices.

Unrealised return compares captured value with invested cost. Cash, other assets, realised trades and dividends are excluded. Missing inputs remain unavailable.

Dividend credits are deduplicated across saved reports and shown separately. Open holdings XIRR uses dated P&L purchase lots only when instrument identities, quantities and costs reconcile with the selected holdings. It excludes sold holdings and dividends; incomplete coverage is unavailable. Daily movement and tax estimates need additional inputs.
