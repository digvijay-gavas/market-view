DATA PROVENANCE

What these figures mean

Values use the price saved in each holdings capture. Refresh reads Drive again; it does not fetch live market prices or capture new holdings. Accounts may have different capture dates. This is a holdings view, excluding cash, other asset classes, realised trades and dividends from its return calculation.

Daily price movement, XIRR and tax estimates are unavailable here: the holdings snapshots do not provide the complete inputs. Dividend credits are deduplicated across saved reports and shown separately, for the selected accounts, across all available dates.
