MARKET / APP INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

About Market

Market is a personal portfolio viewer operated by Digvijay Gavas. It reads saved Market data from a Google Drive folder you choose and calculates summaries in your browser. Contact: digvijay.gavas@gmail.com.

Google data access

When you connect Google Drive, Market requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. This permission can read all Drive files accessible to your signed-in account; it is not restricted by Google to one folder. The app requests the chosen store’s folder listings, catalog, saved holdings, P&L purchase lots, dividend reports and confirmation logs. It uses these only to display and calculate your portfolio. It does not create, edit, upload or delete Drive files or place trades.

Where information is processed

Portfolio file contents and calculations are processed in your browser. The app sends authenticated read requests directly to Google Drive. It does not send portfolio contents or Google tokens to an application backend, analytics service, advertising service or AI training service. The operator does not automatically receive your portfolio through the app. Data you independently choose to share in a support message is separate from this processing.

Browser storage and retention

A short-lived Google access token, its expiry and the selected folder are kept in this tab’s sessionStorage to restore a valid session after reload. Portfolio snapshots, file revisions and downloaded file contents are cached in IndexedDB within a random sign-in scope. Disconnect clears the active session and its portfolio cache. Expired cache records are no longer used and are purged when the database next opens. Your public OAuth client ID and account-selection preferences may be saved in localStorage until you clear site data. A configured PIN unlocks an encrypted public client ID; the PIN is not committed to the repository or stored by the app. No Google refresh token or client secret is stored.

Sharing and third-party services

Market does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google provides authentication and Drive storage. GitHub Pages hosts the static application and policy pages; hosting providers can process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and browser headers under their own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Your controls and deletion

Use Disconnect to clear the active app session and its portfolio cache. Use your browser’s site-data controls to remove all saved selections and cached data for this site. Disconnect does not delete your original Drive files, sign you out of Google globally or revoke Google consent. You can revoke access in Google Account third-party connections. Each user must authorize their own Google account, and Drive permissions determine which files can be read.

Google API use

Market’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used for the portfolio-viewing functions described here, not advertising, credit decisions or training generalized AI models.

Updates and contact

This policy describes the currently published application. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. For questions about the app or its data handling, contact digvijay.gavas@gmail.com.
